About

I'm a product leader and entrepreneur with ~20 years of experience building technology products across geographies. I'm energized by building products at scale which users love and find useful. After a long career at Microsoft working on products like Bing Ads and Microsoft Teams across the US, Singapore, India, and Sweden, I founded Prodloop — a Voice AI intelligence platform for enterprises.
